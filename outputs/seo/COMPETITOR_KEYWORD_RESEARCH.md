## Original content opportunities requiring approval

Gather actual engagement inputs, agreed outputs, target-market selection criteria, representation boundaries, introduction/follow-up process and questions heard from real prospects. Add sector/market detail only where expertise and availability are confirmed. Prepare case studies with written client permissions, documented scope, timeframe and defensible outcomes; anonymize only with approval. Verify existing logo permissions. Publish useful original insights with author/reviewer identity and real dates; an empty content calendar does not justify indexable placeholder articles.

Keep homepage branding, titles, breadcrumbs and navigation consistent. These support understanding and potential sitelinks; selection is automated.
