## Measurement definitions and targets

- Technical target: all 16 intended routes accessible, canonical and index-eligible; zero accidentally indexed preview/draft pages observed. Google may choose not to index every eligible page.
- Search baseline: Search Console property/date range, country/device filter, branded query definition and service page groups saved with each export. CTR = clicks/impressions; average position is an aggregate metric, not a universal rank.
- Business indicator: count enquiries the business qualifies as relevant, using a documented definition. Measure aggregate counts only; do not expose prospect details in public reports. Distinguish browser tests, spam and genuine enquiries.
- Performance targets: field p75 LCP <=2.5 seconds, INP <=200 milliseconds, CLS <=0.1. If CrUX has insufficient data, report that explicitly and use repeatable laboratory checks while gathering field evidence through approved means.
- Authority evidence: genuine current profile/member/partner references with consistent business identity and the official URL. Record permissions and provenance. Confirm Google Business Profile eligibility before considering one; do not invent an office or treat an unrelated Veda listing as this business.

Owner approves new claims, published articles, real testimonials, biographies and markets. SEO work cannot guarantee first-page positions or sitelinks. Navigation and unique substantive service pages already support the practical foundation; credible identity and useful original evidence are the next growth constraints.
